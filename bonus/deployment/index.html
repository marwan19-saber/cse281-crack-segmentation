CSE281 course project, Alamein International University

Measure the damage.

Give it a photo of a concrete wall or slab. It marks every crack in red, measures how much of the surface is cracked, and rates the damage as minor, moderate or severe. Everything runs on your own device: the photo is never sent anywhere.

How the cracks were found

The photo passes through these stages in order. Select one to see it full size.

Method

  1. Smooth away surface texture
  2. Even out shadows and dark corners
  3. Mark pixels darker than their surroundings
  4. Join broken pieces and fill small gaps
  5. Keep long, thin shapes; drop round stains

How well it works

On 137 photos it never saw during development, the marked cracks overlap the hand-drawn ones by 67 % on average (IoU 0.67), against 45 % for a simple darkness threshold. No machine learning is involved: every step is classical image processing.

Where it fails

Rough, pitted concrete can produce false crack pieces, and very wide spalled cracks are found only at their edges, as the wide-crack example shows. This is a student project, not a certified inspection: an unmarked area is not proof of no damage.
