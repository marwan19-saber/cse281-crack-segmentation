Concrete crack segmentation and measurement

Upload a photo of a concrete surface. A classical image-processing pipeline (Gaussian smoothing, frequency-domain illumination flattening, adaptive threshold, gap linking, morphology, shape-based selection) marks the cracks and reports the cracked area, length and a severity class. It runs entirely in your browser with Python (Pyodide); no image is uploaded to any server. Course project (CSE281), not a certified inspection tool.
