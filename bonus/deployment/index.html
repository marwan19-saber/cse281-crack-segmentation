3 · How the result was made

Every stage of the pipeline, in order. Select one to show it above.

The pipeline

  1. Gaussian smoothing (7×7) against surface texture
  2. Illumination flattening with a frequency-domain low-pass
  3. Adaptive threshold: darker than the local mean − 15
  4. Gap linking, closing and small-hole filling
  5. Keep long, thin objects (stains and pores are round)

Severity classes

From the cracked-area percentage, with cut-offs from the development set.

minor below 1.00 % moderate 1.00 – 1.37 % severe 1.37 % and above

Limits

Rough, pitted surfaces can produce false crack pieces; very wide spalled cracks are found only at their edges (see the "known failure" example). This is a course project, not a certified inspection tool: a missed crack never means "no damage".
