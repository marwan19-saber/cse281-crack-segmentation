II. Dataset and Image Analysis

A. Dataset

[Draft facts, verified 12 Nov 2026] We use the Concrete Crack Segmentation Dataset [3]: 458 RGB photographs of concrete surfaces in buildings at the Middle East Technical University, each with a binary crack mask (CC BY 4.0). 437 images are 4032×3024 and 20 are 3264×2448. Cracks cover 1.36% of the pixels on average, and every image contains a crack. The masks are stored as JPEG and are binarized at 127.

Owner: MEM1. Source, licence, citation, number of images, GT type, formats and sizes. Dev/test split (counts, seed, grouping). Labelling procedure and second-member check, if the team made labels. Ethics note if the domain is sensitive.
